Hi, I am Ashutosh. I like the world, its mysteries, and attempting to understand them. 

On the work front: I got a PhD working on quantum information and cryptography in 2025. Upon its compeletion, I worked at <a href="https://www.ingonyama.com/" target="_blank">Ingonyama</a> as a cryptography researcher and then as an AI researcher as the company transitioned to <a href="https://www.moonmath.ai/" target="_blank">Moonmath.ai</a>.

I am presently looking for job opportunities in research or engineering, where I can contribute to solving hard and important problems.

## Education

I completed my PhD in Computer Science in the field of quantum information theory and cryptography under the supervision of <a href="https://www.iro.umontreal.ca/~dupuisf/" target="_blank">Frédéric Dupuis</a> at the University of Montreal. My thesis titled _An exploration of approximation chains_ identified approximation structures which frequently occur in quantum protocols and developed techniques for their analysis.

## Selected Projects

1. [<b>BackLite</b>](https://github.com/moonmath-ai/BackLite): A novel algorithm to dynamically discover and exploit the sparsity of the attention matrix in order to speed up the backward pass while mathematically approximating the attention gradients. Developed at Moonmath. 
   - [Blog post](https://zro.moonmath.ai/posts/introducing-backlite)
1. [<b>ICICLE-LABRADoR</b>](https://github.com/ingonyama-zk/icicle-labrador): A simple, clear and highly performant implementation of the lattice-based zkSNARK, LaBRADOR. Developed at Ingonyama.
   - [Blog post](https://hackmd.io/@Ingonyama/fast-labrador-prover)
1. [<b>Halo2-svd</b>](https://github.com/goforashutosh/halo2-svd): A library for singular value decomposition (SVD) verification and matrix operations in Halo2. Developed as a part of Axiom Open Source Program (Oct 2023).
   - [Blog post](https://hackmd.io/@SQko9rCYRT67XG7dx6zaRQ/r1cWTJEfT)
   - Error analysis for SVD verification: [pdf](https://github.com/goforashutosh/halo2-svd/blob/svd/Error%20Analysis%20for%20SVD.pdf)
1. [<b>secretBallot</b>](https://github.com/goforashutosh/secretBallot): A single stage private voting application with recursive vote aggregation on MINA blockchain. Developed during ZKP/Web3 hackathon organized by UC Berkeley (May 2023).
